# Privacy Policy for Cardo

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Overview
Cardo ("the App") is a loyalty card wallet application developed by RIODnmfp ("the Developer"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection
**Cardo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

All your data stays on your device. Specifically:

- **Card data** (store names, card numbers, barcodes, notes, expiry dates) is stored locally on your device only.
- **Store logos** (photos you take or select) are stored locally on your device only.
- **App preferences** (appearance settings, notification preferences) are stored locally on your device only.

## Camera and Photo Library Access
The App requests access to:

- **Camera** — to scan barcodes and QR codes on your loyalty cards, and to take photos of store logos. Camera data is processed on-device and is never transmitted externally.
- **Photo Library** — to allow you to select store logo images. Selected images are copied into the App's local storage and are not accessed again from your photo library.

## Notifications
The App may send local notifications to remind you of expiring loyalty cards. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external server.

## Update Checks
The App periodically checks for available updates by contacting a publicly hosted file on GitHub. This check transmits no personal data — it only retrieves version information. No identifying information about you or your device is sent during this check.

## Analytics
When distributed through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect anonymised, aggregated analytics data (such as install counts, crash reports, and session data) in accordance with Apple's own privacy policy. The Developer does not have access to any individually identifiable data through this service.

## Data Sharing
Cardo does not share any data with third parties. There are no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking tools embedded in the App.

## Backup and Export
The App offers an export feature that saves your card data to a JSON file on your device. You control where this file is saved and shared. The Developer never has access to your exported data.

## Data Security
Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device encryption and, if enabled, biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID).

## Children's Privacy
Cardo does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal data from any user, it is safe for use by all ages.

## Changes to This Policy
This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
